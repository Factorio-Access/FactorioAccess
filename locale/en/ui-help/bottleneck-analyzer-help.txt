The bottleneck analyzer tells you which ingredients your crafting machines are waiting for. Every assembling machine and rocket silo is checked once every 5 seconds. Furnaces are left out, because they choose their recipe from what they are given, and so is fuel.

Each row is an ingredient, the one keeping the most machines waiting first. A row says how many machines it kept waiting on average, how many machines were short of it at some point, and how much they were missing per craft when short.

Move along a row to hear the machines short of that ingredient: what each is making, how much of the time it was short, and where it is. Machines on the surface you are viewing come first, nearest the cursor first. Click a machine to move the cursor to it, viewing its surface if it is on another one.

Press U on a machine for the production statistics of the ingredient it is missing, on that machine's surface.

Control Tab changes the time range: the last 30 seconds, the last minute or the last 10 minutes. Tab changes what is counted: all surfaces, the machines under the cursor when the cursor is bigger than one tile, then each surface on its own.

The numbers are read when you open the analyzer, and do not change while it is open. To see newer numbers, close it and open it again.
